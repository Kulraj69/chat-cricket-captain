# Ideas (odds are estimates)
1. **Chat Cricket Captain** (PICK). Chat captains a live T20 over: each ball, chat votes a shot (!drive !pull !sweep !leave !six). The overlay animates the ball, a commentator line, and the scoreboard. Channel-point redeems trigger a "DRS review"; a raid starts a "Super Over". Every chatter gets a run/wicket stat, and there's an MVP leaderboard. It's novel, fun to watch, and has an India twist that still works for anyone. Est. finalist odds 5–9%.
2. Chat Heist Planner: chat votes roles and routes in a heist and the overlay plays it out. Fun, but crowd-vote games are a common category. 3–6%.
3. Backseat Oracle: an AI that guesses streamer outcomes and chat bets points on them. Too close to Twitch Predictions. 2–4%.
4. Emote Weather: a generative overlay whose weather changes with chat emote sentiment. Pretty, but there's little interaction. 2–4%.
5. Jugaad Workshop: chat suggests absurd fixes and an AI ranks them. Funny, but hard to make reliable live. 2–4%.
